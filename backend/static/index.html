Pipeline

How it works

Five stages, from a dashcam frame to a ranked work order.

1

Detect

YOLOv8s finds longitudinal, transverse and alligator cracks and potholes (RDD2022 classes D00, D10, D20, D40).

2

Score

Severity 0–10 from damage type and how much of the frame each box covers. Low, Medium, High bands.

3

Weight

Each segment's road class sets its traffic volume. A highway defect outranks the same defect on a lane.

4

Optimize

0/1 knapsack: choose the repairs that remove the most risk without exceeding the budget.

5

Plan

Ranked repair list, map, unfunded trade-offs with reasons, and the risk curve across budgets.

# per segment severity = min(10, 20 × Σ wclass × box_area) cost = ₹15,000 + Σ unit_costclass × area_m² traffic = log(AADT) / log(30,000) risk = severity × traffic # network maximise Σ riski · xi subject to Σ costi · xi ≤ budget, xi ∈ {0,1}

Model comparison

mAP@50

Per class, best model

1024 px
What these numbers mean. Our own YOLOv8s, fine-tuned for 18 epochs on free Colab, reached 0.138. A public RDD2022 YOLOv8s checkpoint scored 0.24 on the same split, and 0.265 when run at 1024 px, because thin cracks survive the higher resolution. The split is the Norway subset: wide frames, small cracks, and only 93 potholes, which the model rarely finds. More pothole data and longer training are the next steps.
