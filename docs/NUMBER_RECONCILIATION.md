## 2. Key Specific Issue Resolutions

### Resolution 1: Sensitivity Win Rate vs. Trial Count
- **Historical Claims in Older Drafts:** Quoted "99.8% win rate vs worst-first over 500 trials".
- **Source Artifact:** `results/sensitivity_summary.json` generated by `scripts/sensitivity_analysis.py`.
- **Empirical Resolution:**
  - In a 500-trial Monte Carlo simulation across $\pm 30\%$ parameter variations:
    - **Strict Win Rate vs. Worst-First:** **46.8%** (Knapsack risk strictly exceeds Worst-First risk).
    - **Tie Rate vs. Worst-First:** **31.4%** (both strategies identify and fund the exact same candidate set).
    - **Win-or-Tie Rate vs. Worst-First:** **78.2%** under randomized continuous budgets ($₹3\text{ L}$ to $₹12\text{ L}$).
    - In 21.8% of random continuous budget trials, continuous greedy packs a marginal item that integer ceiling discretization ($c_i = \lceil \text{cost}_i / 1000 \rceil$, $B = \lfloor \text{budget} / 1000 \rfloor$) rejects by a fraction of an integer unit.
    - Under identical discrete budget constraints, Knapsack is **100% optimal**. In fixed budget tests ($₹5.0\text{ L}$), win-or-tie rate is **99.8%**.
    - **Strict Win Rate vs. Complaint-Order:** **71.6%** (Win-or-tie: **98.0%**), delivering an average risk elimination gain of **+8.11 percentage points**.
- **Status:** **CORRECTED**. All documents now explicitly report the strict win rate (46.8%), tie rate (31.4%), win-or-tie rate (78.2%), and explain the mathematical discretization boundary.

### Resolution 2: Price of Fairness and Spatial Gini Index
- **Historical Claims in Older Drafts:** Quoted "Price of Fairness 6.4%" and "Gini decreased from 0.42 to 0.18" without an empirical calculation script.
- **Source Artifact:** `results/equity_analysis.json` generated by `scripts/equity_check.py`.
- **Empirical Resolution:**
  - Evaluated on a 30-segment benchmark network partitioned into 3 OpenStreetMap civic zones in Kochi ($₹5.0\text{ L}$ budget):
    - Zone 1 (Fort Kochi / Mattancherry): $₹221,892$ need
    - Zone 2 (Ernakulam Central): $₹137,292$ need
    - Zone 4 (Thoppumpady / South): $₹561,532$ need
  - **Standard Knapsack (Unconstrained):** Achieves **64.37%** risk reduction. Zone funding rates: Zone 1 = 45.9%, Zone 2 = 71.6%, Zone 4 = 52.2%.
  - **Fair Knapsack (25% Floor Constraint):** Under consistent discrete capacity tracking (500 units of ₹1,000), standard knapsack already funds $\ge 45.9\%$ in every zone, satisfying the 25% floor non-bindingly. Both select the identical 13 segments (64.37% risk reduction).
  - **Price of Fairness:** **0.00 percentage points** (Standard and Fair knapsack achieve identical optimal risk reduction).
  - **Spatial Gini Coefficient (on % of need funded):**
    - Standard Knapsack: **0.101**
    - Fair Knapsack (25% floor): **0.101** (identical plan under non-binding 25% floor).
    - When a binding floor (e.g., 50%) is tested, Gini drops to 0.047 with Price of Fairness = +1.65 percentage points.
- **Status:** **CORRECTED**. The previous negative Price of Fairness (-0.2% pts) was diagnosed as an artifact of inconsistent discrete unit tracking in Phase 2. Both now use strictly consistent discrete budget units, bounding fair knapsack by the unconstrained optimum.

### Resolution 3: Repair Rate Card by Damage Class
- **Historical Claims in Older Drafts:** Inconsistent mentions of flat repair costs (e.g. ₹3,000 per pothole, ₹15,000 per crack).
- **Source Artifact:** `config.json` and `winterroad_config.py`.
- **Empirical Resolution:**
  - Standardized PWD rate card is strictly surface area-based plus fixed mobilization:
    $$\text{Cost} = \text{Fixed Mobilization (₹15,000)} + \sum (\text{Unit Rate}_c \times \text{Area}_c \times 20\text{ m}^2 \times 10)$$
  - **D00 (Longitudinal Crack):** **₹600 / m²**
  - **D10 (Transverse Crack):** **₹600 / m²**
  - **D20 (Alligator Crack):** **₹1,200 / m²**
  - **D40 (Pothole):** **₹2,500 / m²**
  - **Fixed Site Mobilization:** **₹15,000 / site visit** (truck dispatch, roller, crew barricading).
- **Status:** **CORRECTED**. All flat defect rates removed; area-based rate card standardized across the entire repository.

### Resolution 4: Facility Bonus Cap
- **Historical Claims in Older Drafts:** Vague or unbounded bonus multipliers.
- **Source Artifact:** `config.json` and `winterroad_config.py`.
- **Empirical Resolution:**
  - Individual proximity multipliers for critical civic infrastructure:
    - **Hospital / Clinic:** **+60%** within **500m** radius (factor = 1.60)
    - **School (K-12):** **+40%** within **300m** radius (factor = 1.40)
    - **College / Higher Ed:** **+30%** within **400m** radius (factor = 1.30)
  - **Cumulative Facility Cap:** Bounded at **+100% maximum boost** (`max_cumulative_boost_pct: 100`, composite multiplier $\le 2.0\times$). In standard test cases, single-facility corridors are tested at **+60%** (factor 1.60x).
- **Status:** **CORRECTED**. Defined and bounded in configuration and documentation.

### Resolution 5: Citizen Report Fusion Breakdown and Clustering Radius
- **Historical Claims in Older Drafts:** "50m DBSCAN clustering saves ₹5.50L".
- **Source Artifact:** `scripts/report_fusion.py` and `results/report_fusion_analysis.json`.
- **Empirical Resolution:**
  - **Clustering Radius:** Implemented as **25.0 meters** greedy spatial clustering (not 50m DBSCAN). 25m aligns with consumer GPS accuracy ($\pm 8\text{m}$) while preventing improper merging across separate street intersections.
  - **Duplicate Complaints:** 50 raw citizen reports condensed to **24 unique physical sites** (52.0% duplicate rate).
  - **Total Financial Savings:** **₹550,779** (48.9% cost reduction).
  - **Disaggregated Savings Breakdown:**
    - **Mobilization Overhead Saved:** **₹390,000** (**70.8%** of total savings) by eliminating 26 redundant contractor truck mobilizations at ₹15,000/site.
    - **Material / Patching Saved:** **₹160,779** (**29.2%** of total savings) by avoiding redundant asphalt paving.
- **Status:** **CORRECTED**. Clustering radius corrected to 25.0m; over 70% of savings attributed transparently to crew mobilization overhead.

### Resolution 6: Norway Crack mAP Benchmark (3-Class Only)
- **Purged Historical Claims:** Legacy 4-class overall average (which averaged the 3 real crack classes with a meaningless N=1 pothole split) and 0.001 pothole mAP.
- **Source Artifact:** `results/detection_metrics.json` and `results/norway_split_counts.json`.
- **Empirical Resolution:**
  - Evaluated on RDD2022 Norway validation split (1,632 images, Roboflow `tdt4265-vd97u`).
  - Class validation breakdown at 1024px:
    - **D00 (Longitudinal Crack):** **0.445 mAP@50** (624 ground-truth annotations)
    - **D10 (Transverse Crack):** **0.293 mAP@50** (298 ground-truth annotations)
    - **D20 (Alligator Crack):** **0.321 mAP@50** (218 ground-truth annotations)
    - **D40 (Pothole):** Only **1 ground-truth annotation** exists across all 1,632 images; excluded from benchmark.
  - **Reported Metric:** **0.353 mAP@50** (3-class macro crack mean: $\frac{0.445 + 0.293 + 0.321}{3} = \mathbf{0.353}$).
  - **Label:** Labeled strictly as **Norway validation, crack classes only (D00, D10, D20)**. The legacy 4-class average has been purged repo-wide.
- **Status:** **CORRECTED**. Legacy 4-class average purged; 3-class 0.353 reported exclusively.
