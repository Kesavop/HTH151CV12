WinterRoad · Municipal Road Prioritization Platform

Too many potholes.
Not enough money.
Fix the right roads first.

Municipalities receive fixed annual road maintenance budgets that cannot cover every defect. WinterRoad detects road surface defects from camera photos, maps them on OpenStreetMap topologies, weights them by traffic and proximity to essential facilities (hospitals and schools), and solves a constrained 0/1 knapsack optimization problem to maximize safety risk reduction.

The Municipal Problem

A city can't fix every road at once

Public works departments operate under strict budgetary ceilings. Today, repair order decisions are often made ad-hoc ("first-come, first-served") or based on informal complaints, leaving critical arterial roads neglected.

1

Budget Caps

Annual funding covers only a fraction of detected road damages. Everything beyond the budget envelope must wait for future quarters.

2

Automated Detection

Deep learning computer vision (YOLOv8) catalogs longitudinal cracks, transverse cracks, alligator cracking, and dangerous potholes from camera images.

3

Risk-Weighted Value

Damage on an arterial road within 500 meters of a major hospital carries far higher public safety risk than a defect on an isolated residential lane.

System Architecture

How WinterRoad Works

From a smartphone road photo to an optimal municipal repair schedule in five reproducible steps.

1

Detect

Multi-class YOLOv8 model classifies defects (D00, D10, D20, D40) with automated face & license plate blurring.

2

Geolocate

GPS coordinates place each defect onto real OpenStreetMap road segments with EXIF tamper verification.

3

Weight Exposure

Traffic volume factors (High / Medium / Low) and proximity bonuses for hospitals and schools compute composite risk.

4

Optimize

0/1 Knapsack dynamic programming algorithm maximizes eliminated risk strictly within the allocated rupee budget.

5

Dispatch

Triage queue, ticket tracking for citizens, and printable work-order contractor schedules for engineers.
