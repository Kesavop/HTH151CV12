Too many potholes.

Gladiators finds road damage from photos, puts it on the real city map, gives extra priority to busy roads and roads near hospitals, schools and colleges, and picks the repairs that remove the most risk within a fixed government budget.

Open the live plannerHow it works
The problem

A city can't fix every road at once

Road departments get a fixed budget every year, but damaged roads always cost more than that. Someone has to decide which roads come first, and today that is often "whoever complained first".

1

Fixed budget

The government gives a set amount. Everything above it waits for next year.

2

Where is the damage?

Cracks and potholes must be found and located on the map before anyone can plan repairs.

3

Which road matters?

A pothole on a busy road near a hospital is more urgent than one on a quiet lane.

Our solution

How it works

Five steps, from a road photo to a ranked repair list.

1

Detect

YOLOv8 finds cracks and potholes in road photos (D00, D10, D20, D40).

2

Locate

The photo's GPS puts each damage on the exact street of the city map.

3

Weight

Traffic level High / Medium / Low from the road type, plus extra priority near hospitals, schools and colleges.

4

Optimize

A 0/1 knapsack picks the repairs that remove the most risk without crossing the budget.

5

Plan

Map, ranked list, and a downloadable dataset for the road department.

How it decides

Traffic level comes from the road class: highways and main roads High, secondary and tertiary roads Medium, residential streets Low.

Risk = damage weight × size × traffic weight (1.0 / 0.7 / 0.4) × public-place factor. A 0/1 knapsack picks the repairs that remove the most risk within the budget.

Dataset

Our road damage dataset

We survey roads with a phone camera (GPS on). YOLO finds the damage in each photo, and every damage point becomes one row. The planner adds the street, traffic level and nearest hospital, school and college.
